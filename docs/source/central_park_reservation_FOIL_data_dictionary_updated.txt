schedule_slot_id: The unique, database-generated identifier for the schedule slot.

court: The court the schedule slot is assigned to.

reservation_time_and_date: The date and time the schedule slot is assigned to, separated
by hour long blocks. A booking is made for this date and time on this slot's court.

schedule_status: The status of the schedule slot, indicating if a booking can be
made at the selected time. The possible statuses are:
	-unbookable
	-unassigned
	-assigned
	-rained-out
	-all-checkedin
	-booking-in-progress

reason: If the booking has been canceled or otherwise unavailable, indicates the reason 
for it provided by the staff member.

creation_timestamp: The date and time the booking is created and assigned to a potential 
player.

player_status: The current status of the player who made the reservation. 
This is separate from the status of the schedule slot. The possible statuses are:
	-Assigned
	-Checked in
	-No Show
	-Canceled
	-Rained Out
	-Booking in Progress
	-Rained Out Rebooked
	-Canceled Rebooked
	-Assigned Rebooked

method_of_reservation: Indicates the method the booking was made. The possible methods are
	-online
	-reservation
	-walkup
	-waiting list
	-repeat list
	-second
	-reservation-phone
	-third
	-fourth
Note that entries listed such as "second", "third" or "fourth" indicate this was an 
additional player being booked on one booking.

permit_type: The type of permit that was used for this booking, Possible types are: 
-Permit
-Single Play
-NP (No permit)

action: Indicates if the schedule slot was canceled or rained out at any point.

cancellation_time: Time of cancellation. Note that the schedule slot may have been
updated once again after the recorded cancellation time.
